Independent judgement (opinion — separate from the cited findings)
Profile information architecture — the 15 profile tabs were over-split and conflated; regrouped to five logical groups (addressed).

1. Org Admin — the tenant configuration hub · GO
Org Admin owns the per-tenant cfg-* configuration console — a dedicated managing screen for nearly every domain (legal entities, business units, departments, designations/grades/bands, workforce classification, regions/locations, custom fields, assignment rules, RBAC roles & permissions, national ID, leave/attendance/performance platform settings, shifts, weekly-off, infraction, goal/review templates, calibration, rating, PIP, hiring leads, external recruiters, sources, duplicity, BGV checklist, document settings, templates, letterheads, signers, workflows, approval flows, forms, skip, SLA, notifications), plus cross-entity reports and the audit log.
Config surface fully audited in the Configuration Ownership & Coverage Map (115 files → 79 Present · 35 Partial · 1 Missing). Org Admin is the home of the overwhelming majority of these screens; functional admins provide the operational, in-context view of the same configuration.
DISC-ORG-001 (cross-ref CFG-MAP-100): the single Missing config — Assign_Assessments_Export.csv (assessment assignment) — has no managing screen. It belongs to Recruitment Admin, not Org Admin; recorded in the handoff index as the one screen to add.
No prototype changes. Org Admin is GO; its coverage is established by the Coverage Map.

Correctly external in scope — no internal/admin pages bleed into the candidate panel; the experience is confined to the candidate’s own onboarding and offer artifacts.
No prototype changes. Candidate is GO.
